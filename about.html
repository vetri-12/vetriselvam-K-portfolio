I work at the intersection of business operations, software architecture, and emerging AI technologies. My expertise lies in understanding complex business challenges and translating them into practical, scalable software solutions.

As a Software Solutions Consultant at Vensanix Global, I manage the end-to-end software delivery lifecycle. My approach starts with deep business analysis—gathering requirements, defining functional workflows, and architecting the solution. I then execute the technical implementation using AI-assisted development (working primarily with Laravel, Filament PHP, and Tailwind CSS), followed by rigorous functional testing and production deployment.

Recently, I architected and deployed a live, custom B2B ERP system for a client in the facade and construction industry, automating their measurement, procurement, and financial workflows. I also develop and manage corporate web presences using WordPress, handling everything from domain configuration to client requirement translation.

My long-term focus is on deepening my technical capabilities in SaaS architecture, cloud infrastructure, and AI integration to build robust, market-facing technology products. I thrive in roles where I can bridge the gap between strategic business goals and technical execution.
